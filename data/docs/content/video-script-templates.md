## VIDEO BEST PRACTICES

### For Short-Form
- Hook in first 1-2 seconds (or viewers scroll)
- Keep text on screen brief
- Use trending audio when relevant
- End with clear CTA
- Vertical format (9:16)

### For Long-Form
- Get to the content quickly (skip long intros)
- Use chapters for easy navigation
- B-roll keeps it interesting
- Address viewer questions proactively
- Horizontal format (16:9)

### For All Videos
- Good lighting (natural or ring light)
- Clean audio (lapel mic recommended)
- Steady footage (tripod or stabilizer)
- Show the product in use, not just sitting there
- Be authentic - don't over-script
