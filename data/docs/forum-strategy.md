# EZ4X4 Forum Strategy Playbook

## Posting Schedule
- **Best time:** Monday 8AM Pacific (11AM Eastern)
- **Peak window:** 8AM-1PM Pacific weekdays
- **Weekends:** Lower activity

## Weekly Cadence
- **3 new threads/week** per forum
- **10 responses/day** in other threads
- Giveaways always in separate threads

## Content Types

### Discussion Threads (Non-Promotional)
- Weather/trail discussions
- "How'd you handle [situation]?"
- Themed days: "Top Off Tuesday", "Freedom Panel Friday"

### Product Threads
- Installed photos (not spammy, just eye candy)
- Link to existing positive review threads
- Photos perform well - iPhone quality is fine

### Giveaways
- Always start NEW threads
- Post in BOTH technical sections AND sponsor section
- Title search "giveaway" on forums for ideas

## Best Practices
- **Wolf Box model:** Tons of giveaways + non-promotional discussions
- **3W Liners model:** Same strategy, very successful
- **Oracle Lighting:** Themed weekly threads

## Rules
- NEVER comment on other sponsors' threads
- Can't coerce positive reviews
- No fake accounts/shilling (IPs tracked)
- Report competitor fake accounts to Jay
