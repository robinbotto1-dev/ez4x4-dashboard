## FAQ for Club Leaders

**Q: Is there any cost for clubs to participate?**
A: No! Everything is free. We just provide discounts for your members.

**Q: What if members have issues with orders?**
A: We handle all customer service. Just have them contact us directly or CC you if needed.

**Q: Can we customize the discount code?**
A: Yes! We'll create a code specific to your club (e.g., BRONCO6G15).

**Q: How do we track how many members used the code?**
A: We'll provide monthly reports showing orders and savings for your members.

**Q: What if we want to run multiple group buys?**
A: No limit! Run as many as you'd like. We recommend quarterly to maximize participation.
