# EZ4X4 Dealership Partnership Strategy

*B2B channel opportunity*

---

## The Opportunity

Most competitors sell direct-to-consumer. Dealerships represent an **untapped B2B channel** that could provide:

- Recurring wholesale orders
- New customer acquisition
- Brand credibility
- Display/showroom presence
- Service department installations

### Phase 1: Research (Week 1-2)

1. Build target list of 100 dealerships
2. Find parts manager/accessories contact
3. Research what they currently carry
4. Note any existing relationships

## Tracking & Goals

### KPIs
- Dealers contacted per week: 20
- Response rate: 10-15%
- Conversion to account: 20% of responses
- Average first order: $1,500
- Reorder rate: 60% within 90 days

### 6-Month Goals
- Month 1: 5 dealer accounts
- Month 2: 10 total accounts
- Month 3: 20 total accounts
- Month 6: 50 total accounts

### Revenue Projection
- 50 dealers x $1,000/month average = $50K/month wholesale
- At 40% margin: $30K gross profit
- Plus: Retail customers they send to ez4x4.com
