## STORAGE OPTIONS COMPARED

### Option 1: Wall Mount Solutions

**Pros:**
- Keeps panels off the floor
- Easy access
- Looks clean in garage

**Cons:**
- Requires wall space
- Not portable
- Drilling required

**Popular options:**
- DIY pegboard/hooks ($20-50)
- Dedicated wall carriers ($80-200)

---

### Option 2: Portable Carriers

**Pros:**
- Take to trail
- No installation needed
- Can store multiple sets

**Cons:**
- Takes up floor space
- Can tip over

**Popular options:**
- Foam inserts in tote ($30)
- Dedicated carriers ($100-250)

---

### Option 3: In-Vehicle Storage

**Pros:**
- Always with you
- Protected during transport
- Space efficient

**Cons:**
- Takes cargo space
- Weight consideration
