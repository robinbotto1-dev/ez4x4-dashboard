## PROGRAM STRUCTURE

### Commission Tiers

| Tier | Requirements | Commission | Perks |
|------|--------------|------------|-------|
| **Starter** | Sign up | 10% | Basic tracking |
| **Active** | $500+ sales/month | 12% | Priority support |
| **Pro** | $2,000+ sales/month | 15% | Free products, early access |
| **Elite** | $5,000+ sales/month | 18% | + Paid collaborations |

### Commission Details
- Paid monthly (Net 30)
- Minimum payout: $50
- Payment via PayPal or direct deposit
- 30-day cookie window

## PROGRAM POLICIES

### Code of Conduct
Affiliates must:
- Disclose affiliate relationship (FTC compliance)
- Not make false claims about products
- Not spam or use deceptive practices
- Not bid on brand keywords in paid ads
- Represent the brand professionally

### Termination Triggers
- FTC disclosure violations
- Fraudulent activity
- Brand misrepresentation
- Competitor conflicts
- Extended inactivity (12+ months)
