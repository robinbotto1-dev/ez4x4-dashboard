## AD TESTING NOTES

### Variables to Test

**Creative:**
- Product-focused vs. lifestyle/action shots
- UGC vs. professional photography
- Video vs. static images
- Carousel vs. single image

**Copy:**
- Problem-focused vs. benefit-focused
- Short vs. long primary text
- Emoji use vs. clean copy
- Price mention vs. no price

**Audience:**
- Bronco owners vs. Jeep owners
- Age brackets (25-34 vs. 35-44 vs. 45+)
- Interest-based (overlanding, camping, off-road)
- Lookalikes from purchasers

### KPIs to Track
- CTR (target: 1.5%+)
- CPC (target: <$1.50)
- ROAS (target: 3x+)
- Add to cart rate
- Purchase conversion rate
