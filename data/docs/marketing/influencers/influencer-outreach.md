# EZ4X4 Influencer/Creator Outreach

*Templates and strategy for UGC partnerships*

---

## Target Creator Profiles

### Tier 1: Micro-Influencers (5K-50K followers)
**Why:** High engagement, affordable, authentic content
**Typical deal:** Free product + commission/affiliate code
**Expected ROI:** 3-5x product cost in sales

**Look for:**
- Bronco or Jeep focus
- Regular posting (3+ times/week)
- Good engagement (3%+ on posts)
- Quality content/photography
- Active community in comments

### Tier 2: Mid-Tier (50K-200K followers)
**Why:** Broader reach, established credibility
**Typical deal:** Free product + flat fee ($200-500) + affiliate code
**Expected ROI:** 2-4x investment

### Tier 3: Macro (200K+)
**Why:** Massive reach, brand building
**Typical deal:** $1,000-5,000 + product
**Expected ROI:** Brand awareness, harder to track direct ROI

## Outreach Templates

### Template 1: Cold DM (Instagram/TikTok)

**For micro-influencers (casual tone)**

```

Love your [BRONCO/JEEP] content - especially that [SPECIFIC POST/VIDEO] you did recently.

I'm with EZ4X4, we make accessories for Broncos and Jeeps. Wondering if you'd be interested in trying our [PRODUCT]?

No strings attached - just think it'd look great on your build. If you love it, maybe we can work together on some content.

Let me know if you're interested and I'll get one shipped out!

- [YOUR NAME]
```

## Where to Find Creators

### Search Methods

1. **Hashtag Research**
   - #Bronco #BroncoBuild #BroncoLife
   - #JeepLife #JeepBuild #JeepWrangler
   - #Overlanding #OffRoad #4x4Life

2. **Competitor Tagged Photos**
   - Check who's posting about competitors
   - They might want to try alternatives

3. **Forum Active Members**
   - Bronco6g, JL Wrangler Forums
   - Members with good build threads

4. **YouTube Search**
   - "Bronco accessories review"
   - "Bronco build series"
   - "Bronco mods"

5. **TikTok Discovery**
   - Search Bronco/Jeep content
   - Check "For You" similar creators

### Tools
- Modash (influencer search)
- HypeAuditor (fake follower check)
- Social Blade (growth trends)

## Content Guidelines for Creators

### We Love:
- Authentic, honest reviews
- Install process content
- Before/after shots
- Action shots (trail, camping, etc.)
- Comparison to what they used before

### We Don't Want:
- Scripted, salesy content
- Stock photo style
- Just a product photo with no context
- Negative comparisons to competitors by name

### Required Elements:
- Tag @ez4x4
- Use affiliate code in caption
- Disclose partnership (FTC compliance)

### Nice to Have:
- Link in bio during posting
- Stories in addition to feed post
- Multiple posts over time

## Red Flags to Avoid

🚩 **Fake followers** - Check engagement rate (below 1% = suspicious)
🚩 **Bought engagement** - Generic comments, no real conversation
🚩 **Off-brand content** - If they've promoted things that don't fit
🚩 **No automotive interest** - Just chasing free stuff
🚩 **Unrealistic demands** - Wanting $1,000 for 5K followers
