## Outreach Strategy

### For Micro-Influencers (Tier 1)
- **Offer:** Free product + small fee ($100-300)
- **Ask:** Unboxing + install video, honest review
- **Format:** Instagram Reels/Stories + potential TikTok
- **Timeline:** 2-3 posts over 30 days

### For Macro-Influencers (Tier 2)
- **Offer:** Free product + higher fee ($500-1000) OR affiliate commission
- **Ask:** Dedicated review video, story features, post tags
- **Format:** YouTube review + Instagram content
- **Timeline:** 1 YouTube video + 3-5 Instagram posts
