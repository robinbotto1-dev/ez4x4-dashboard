## BOOTH SETUP

### Layout Principles
1. **Open entrance** - No barriers to entry
2. **Products at eye level** - Easy to see and touch
3. **Demo area** - Let people interact
4. **Clear pricing** - No hunting for prices
5. **Checkout area** - Obvious and accessible

### Visual Hierarchy
```
[BANNER - Brand name/logo visible from distance]

[HERO PRODUCT - Center, eye level]

[SECONDARY PRODUCTS - Flanking]

[DEMO AREA - Interactive space]

[CHECKOUT - Side or back]
```

### Must-Haves
- Products people can touch
- At least one "wow" piece
- Clear branding visible from 20+ feet
- Price tags on everything
- Easy path through booth

## SHOW EXECUTION

### Opening Routine
1. Arrive 30 min before show opens
2. Quick inventory count
3. Test POS system
4. Brief team on day's goals
5. Assume positions before doors open

### Engagement Approach

**The Greeting (not "Can I help you?")**
- "Hey! What do you drive?"
- "Have you seen these before?"
- "What's your setup like?"

**The Qualification**
- "Are you looking to add [capability]?"
- "What's been your biggest frustration with [problem]?"
- "Are you planning any mods this year?"

**The Demo**
- Let them touch it
- Show the quality difference
- Explain the install process
- Address objections proactively

**The Close**
- "We've got show pricing today - [discount]"
- "I can get you set up right now"
- "What questions can I answer?"

### Handling Common Objections

**"I'll think about it"**
→ "Totally understand. Here's my card. But heads up - show pricing ends today and shipping from our site adds $[X]. Want me to hold one for you while you walk around?"

**"I can find it cheaper online"**
→ "You might find something that looks similar, but these are patented designs you won't find elsewhere. Plus no shipping today, and I can answer any questions right now."

**"I need to measure my truck first"**
→ "No problem - what year and model? [Check compatibility] You're good! These are designed specifically for [vehicle]."

**"My spouse needs to approve"**
→ "Smart move. Give them a call - I'll answer any questions they have. Or take a brochure and here's my card."

### Closing Routine
1. Last push 30 min before close
2. Final inventory count
3. Secure high-value items
4. Organize for next day
5. Team debrief (what worked, what didn't)

## SHOW CALCULATOR

### Break-Even Analysis

**Fixed Costs:**
- Booth fee: $____
- Travel: $____
- Lodging: $____
- Meals: $____
- Materials: $____
- **Total Fixed:** $____

**Variable Costs (per unit):**
- Product cost: $____
- Credit card fees (~3%): $____

**Break-Even Units:**
Total Fixed Costs ÷ (Avg Price - Variable Cost) = Units needed

**Example:**
$5,000 fixed ÷ ($200 avg - $80 cost) = 42 units to break even
