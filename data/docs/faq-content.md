## PRODUCT CARE

### How do I clean my [product]?

Most products can be cleaned with mild soap and water. Rinse thoroughly and dry completely.

**Do NOT use:**
- Harsh chemicals
- Abrasive scrubbers
- High-pressure washers directly on mounting points

For powder-coated products, occasional wax application helps maintain finish.

---

### Will your products rust?

Our products are designed for outdoor use with:
- Powder-coated steel (rust-resistant)
- Marine-grade hardware
- Corrosion-resistant treatments

While highly resistant to rust, we recommend rinsing off road salt and debris, especially in winter climates.

---

### How do I maintain my bed rack?

1. **Monthly:** Visual inspection of bolts and mounting points
2. **Quarterly:** Tighten any loose hardware
3. **Annually:** Check for wear and re-torque all fasteners
4. **As needed:** Touch up any paint chips to prevent rust

Most importantly: Don't exceed the weight capacity, and distribute loads evenly.

## TECHNICAL QUESTIONS

### What's the weight capacity of your bed rack?

Our bed racks are rated for:
- **Static load:** 500-800 lbs (parked/stationary)
- **Dynamic load:** 200-300 lbs (while driving)

Check your specific product page for exact ratings. Dynamic loads should be lower because of the forces involved while driving.

---

### Can I use [product] with my tonneau cover?

Compatibility varies by tonneau cover style:
- **Soft roll-up:** Usually compatible
- **Tri-fold:** Usually compatible
- **Retractable:** May require modification
- **One-piece:** Often not compatible

Contact us with your specific cover model and we'll confirm.

---

### Will your products void my vehicle warranty?

Our products are designed to install without permanent modifications to your vehicle. Properly installed aftermarket accessories should not void your vehicle warranty.

Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, dealers cannot void your warranty simply because you installed aftermarket parts - they'd need to prove the part caused the specific failure.

That said, we recommend:
- Following installation instructions exactly
- Keeping documentation of proper installation
- Consulting your dealer if concerned
