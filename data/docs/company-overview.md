# EZ4x4 Master Company File

## Company Overview

### Mission
We make cool accessories for Jeep, Bronco, and pickup trucks. We design and engineer our own stuff. High quality, well thought design, and most importantly we make things that are useful. When people see our products they say "why didn't I think of that."

### Vision
Break into the larger truck accessories space. Currently 50/50 Jeep/Bronco, want to take a bite out of the truck market.

### Core Values
- American engineered products
- Solve problems people have
- Build things people didn't know they wanted
- Original ideas - no ripping from competitors
- Patent when possible

### Origin Story
Founded ~2019 by Brian Goldwitz, an inventor who dropped out of high school to run retail stores (The Wild Side, TeePee Dashery - single stores doing millions/day in revenue). Built folding goals company, tubing company, shipping company, then a factory in China. Started creating Jeep/Bronco accessories and EZ4X4 was born with the patented Fliptop.

## Key Patents & Differentiators

### Tailgate Locking System
- Slip products into crevice between tailgate door and bed
- Close tailgate = locks into place
- Used for Truck Buddiez (movable tie-down points)
- Used for Quick Box (removable bed box using tailgate as door)

### Products Using Patents
- **Fliptop** - Hinge for freedom panels, flip up for open air, flip down to close
- **Truck Buddiez** - Tie-down points using tailgate lock system
- **Quick Box** - Bed box that slides in/out, uses tailgate as door
- **Bed Extender** - Converts to secure bed box
