## EASY WINS - Ranked by Impact & Effort

### 🔥 HIGH IMPACT / LOW EFFORT

#### 1. Sitewide Discount Code Banner
**Who does it:** Bestop ("15% OFF WITH CODE - OPENAIR15")
**Implementation:** Add sticky banner at top of site with current promo code
**Why it works:** Creates urgency, easy to track conversions, gives people a reason to buy NOW
**EZ4X4 action:** Create a permanent 10% code displayed site-wide

#### 2. Vehicle Year/Make/Model Selector
**Who does it:** ALL competitors (Bestop, Quadratec, Hooke Road)
**Implementation:** Dropdown on homepage that filters to compatible products
**Why it works:** Solves your "too many variants" problem, reduces friction, prevents wrong purchases
**EZ4X4 action:** Add YMM selector - this is table stakes for auto accessories

#### 3. Free Shipping Threshold
**Who does it:** Bestop ($99+), most competitors
**Implementation:** Clear messaging in header + checkout
**Why it works:** Increases AOV (people add items to hit threshold)
**EZ4X4 action:** Set threshold at $150-200 based on your $364 AOV

#### 4. Customer Review Carousel on Homepage
**Who does it:** Bestop, Hooke Road ("from 2060 reviews")
**Implementation:** Pull best reviews, display with star ratings and product names
**Why it works:** Social proof, builds trust instantly
**EZ4X4 action:** Add review widget/carousel to homepage

#### 5. Military/First Responder Discount
**Who does it:** Quadratec
**Implementation:** 10% discount with ID.me or SheerID verification
**Why it works:** Builds goodwill, targets demographic likely to own trucks/Jeeps, shareable
**EZ4X4 action:** Easy 10% discount, announce on social

### 💪 HIGH IMPACT / MEDIUM EFFORT

#### 6. Combo/Bundle Deals Section
**Who does it:** Hooke Road ("Combo Kits Deal")
**Implementation:** Dedicated collection page with pre-built bundles
**Why it works:** You mentioned bundles are underperforming - make them more visible
**EZ4X4 action:** Create "Build Kits" or "Starter Packs" landing page

#### 7. Blog/Content Marketing
**Who does it:** ALL competitors
- Bestop: "Caring for Your Hardtop" guides
- Quadratec: "Torque" blog + Jeep Knowledge Center
- Hooke Road: "Roll Bar vs. Bed Rack: Which Is Best?" guides

**Why it works:** SEO, establishes authority, content for social/email
**EZ4X4 action:** Start with 4 cornerstone posts:
- "Freedom Panel Guide: Everything You Need to Know"
- "Truck Bed Organization: Complete Setup Guide"  
- "Bronco vs Jeep Accessories: What Works for Both"
- "Tie-Down Anchor Systems Compared"

#### 8. Sponsorship/Influencer Application Form
**Who does it:** Hooke Road ("Hooke Road Sponsorship Application")
**Implementation:** Simple form collecting: name, social handles, follower count, content type, vehicle
**Why it works:** Formalizes what you're already doing, creates pipeline
**EZ4X4 action:** Add page at /pages/ambassador-apply (you have /ambassador-form already - make it more visible)

#### 9. Group Buys for Clubs
**Who does it:** Hooke Road ("Group Buys for Jeep Club")
**Implementation:** Landing page + form for club organizers to request bulk pricing
**Why it works:** Bronco/Jeep clubs are HUGE, one relationship = many sales
**EZ4X4 action:** Reach out to Bronco6g club leaders, offer 15% group discount

#### 10. "Share Your Build" UGC Collection
**Who does it:** Hooke Road ("Share Your Mods Ideas & Builds Story")
**Implementation:** Landing page with form/hashtag to submit photos
**Why it works:** Free content, social proof, community building
**EZ4X4 action:** Create #EZ4X4Build campaign, feature best submissions

### 📈 MEDIUM IMPACT / LOW EFFORT

#### 11. Installation Guides/Manuals Section
**Who does it:** Hooke Road (dedicated page)
**Implementation:** PDF downloads or video links for each product
**Why it works:** Reduces support tickets, builds confidence to purchase
**EZ4X4 action:** Compile existing install videos into dedicated page

#### 12. Live Chat Widget
**Who does it:** Bestop, Quadratec
**Implementation:** Tidio, Intercom, or Shopify Chat
**Why it works:** Captures leads who have questions, can automate FAQs
**EZ4X4 action:** Start with hours-limited chat to Brian's phone

#### 13. Newsletter Signup Incentive
**Who does it:** ALL competitors
- Bestop: "GET EXCLUSIVE DEALS AND LATEST RELEASES"
- Quadratec: "Subscribe for Exclusive Offers, New Product Releases"
**Implementation:** Popup or footer with discount code for signup
**Why it works:** Builds email list for remarketing
**EZ4X4 action:** Offer 5% off first order for email signup

#### 14. "Track Your Order" Feature
**Who does it:** Hooke Road
**Implementation:** Link to tracking page or app
**Why it works:** Reduces "where's my order" support requests
**EZ4X4 action:** Add prominent link in header + order confirmation emails
