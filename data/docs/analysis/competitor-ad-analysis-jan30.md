## Gaps & Opportunities for EZ4X4

### What Competitors ARE Doing (That Works)
1. **Video content** - Bestop's video ads dominate engagement
2. **Lifestyle messaging** - "Open-air freedom", adventure, escape
3. **Product hero shots** - Clear, well-lit product imagery
4. **Seasonal promos** - Winter deals, free shipping thresholds
5. **Partner/influencer content** - Two Guys Garage, Truck U partnerships

### What Competitors AREN'T Doing
1. **User-generated content** - No visible UGC in ads (EZ4X4's strength!)
2. **Community focus** - No forum/community engagement messaging
3. **Problem/solution format** - Mostly aspirational, not pain-point focused
4. **Installation content** - Limited how-to/install visibility in ads
5. **Comparison ads** - No direct competitor callouts

### Recommended Actions

1. **Double down on UGC** - Competitors aren't showing real customer content
2. **Problem/solution ads** - "Tired of removing your freedom panels?" (Bestop uses this, but EZ4X4 could own it)
3. **Installation confidence** - Show how easy EZ4X4 products are to install
4. **Forum presence** - Neither competitor is pushing community; this is EZ4X4's turf
5. **Price anchoring** - If competing against Bestop's premium ($$$), show value proposition
