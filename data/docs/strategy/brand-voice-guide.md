## VOICE CHARACTERISTICS

### 1. Knowledgeable but Accessible
We know our stuff, but we explain it so anyone can understand.

**Do:** "This rack handles 500 lbs static load - that's your rooftop tent, gear, and then some."
**Don't:** "The static load capacity is 500 lbs per FMVSS standards."

### 2. Confident but Not Arrogant
We stand behind our products without putting others down.

**Do:** "Our products are built to last. That's why we offer a lifetime warranty."
**Don't:** "Unlike cheap Chinese knockoffs, our products actually work."

### 3. Helpful First, Sales Second
Lead with value. The sale follows.

**Do:** "Here's how to solve your panel storage problem. [Then: here's our solution]"
**Don't:** "BUY NOW! Best panel carrier ever! Limited time!"

### 4. Enthusiast, Not Corporate
We're fellow owners, not a faceless company.

**Do:** "We built this because we were tired of scratching our own panels."
**Don't:** "EZ4X4 is committed to delivering innovative solutions for the automotive accessory market."

## WRITING STYLE

### Sentence Structure
- Keep sentences short
- One idea per sentence
- Vary length for rhythm
- Break up long paragraphs

### Formatting
- Use bullet points for features
- Use bold for emphasis (sparingly)
- Use headers to organize
- White space is your friend

### Punctuation
- Periods for statements
- Em dashes for asides—like this
- Exclamation points sparingly!
- Avoid semicolons; use periods instead

### Numbers
- Spell out one through nine
- Use numerals for 10+
- Always use numerals for specs (500 lbs, 15%)
- Use commas in thousands (1,500)

- Synergy
- Solutions

## GRAMMAR & STYLE

### We vs. I
- "We" for company communications
- "I" for personal outreach (sales, support)
- "You" as much as possible (customer focus)

### Active vs. Passive
**Active (preferred):** "We ship orders within 24 hours."
**Passive (avoid):** "Orders are shipped within 24 hours."

### Contractions
Use them! They're more natural.
- We're, you'll, it's, don't, can't
- Makes copy feel conversational

### Oxford Comma
Yes, use it. "Broncos, Jeeps, and trucks."

## BRAND TERMINOLOGY

### Product Names
- Always capitalize product names
- "Freedom Panel Carrier" not "freedom panel carrier"
- "EZFLIP Bed Rack" not "EZ flip bed rack"

### Vehicle Names
- Ford Bronco (full name on first mention)
- Bronco (subsequent mentions okay)
- Jeep Wrangler, Jeep Gladiator
- Never "a Bronco" without context

### Technical Terms
- Rooftop tent (spell out, not RTT, on first mention)
- Year/make/model or YMM (explain acronyms)
- Powder-coated (hyphenated)
- No-drill (hyphenated when used as adjective)

## DO'S AND DON'TS

### Do
✓ Be helpful before being promotional
✓ Use "you" and "your" often
✓ Show the product in real use
✓ Acknowledge problems honestly
✓ Thank customers genuinely
✓ Use specific details over vague claims

### Don't
✗ Bash competitors by name
✗ Use ALL CAPS for emphasis
✗ Over-promise on timelines
✗ Use corporate buzzwords
✗ Ignore negative feedback
✗ Copy competitor messaging

## EXAMPLE REWRITES

### Too Corporate
❌ "EZ4X4 is pleased to announce the launch of our revolutionary new Freedom Panel Carrier solution, designed to provide customers with a premium storage experience."

✓ "We made a better way to store your freedom panels. No scratches, no hassle. Check it out."

### Too Salesy
❌ "🔥🔥🔥 AMAZING DEAL!!! Best panel carrier EVER! Buy now before they're GONE!! 🔥🔥🔥"

✓ "Our Freedom Panel Carrier is back in stock. 15% off this week with code PANELS15."

### Too Vague
❌ "Our products are high-quality and designed for performance."

✓ "Our Freedom Panel Carrier has a padded interior that prevents scratches. We've tested it with over 500 installs."
