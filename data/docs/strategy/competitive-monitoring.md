## COMPETITIVE RESPONSE GUIDELINES

### When Competitor Lowers Price
**Don't:** Immediately match
**Do:** Evaluate if it's sustainable, check if quality decreased, emphasize our value

### When Competitor Launches Similar Product
**Don't:** Panic or copy their messaging
**Do:** Highlight our patents, quality, warranty, reviews

### When Competitor Gets Bad Reviews
**Don't:** Bash them publicly
**Do:** Reach out to those reviewers, offer our solution

### When Competitor Does Something Well
**Don't:** Ignore it
**Do:** Learn from it, adapt good ideas to our brand
