# EZ4X4 Customer Personas

*Who's buying and why*

---

## Persona 1: "Weekend Warrior" Wayne

### Demographics
- **Age:** 35-45
- **Income:** $80-120K household
- **Location:** Suburban, Sun Belt states
- **Vehicle:** 2021+ Bronco 4-door, Big Bend or Outer Banks trim

### Psychographics
- Family man, 1-2 kids
- Bought the Bronco as his "fun car"
- Takes family camping 4-6 times per year
- Watches YouTube reviews before buying anything
- Values quality but is price-conscious

### Behaviors
- Researches extensively before purchasing
- Reads Bronco6g forums but rarely posts
- Follows Bronco accounts on Instagram
- Bought Bronco accessories at dealer, disappointed with quality
- Willing to pay more for "buy it for life" products

### Goals
- Make his Bronco more capable for camping trips
- Keep the family comfortable and organized
- Not look like a "mall crawler"
- Protect his investment (Bronco + accessories)

### Pain Points
- Freedom panels scratched when stored in garage
- Cargo shifts around in the bed
- Doesn't want to drill holes in his new truck
- Limited time for complicated installs

### How EZ4X4 Helps
- No-drill installation = peace of mind
- Quality products = buy once, done
- Quick installs = weekend project, not weekend commitment

### Marketing Messages That Resonate
- "Protect your investment"
- "Install in under 30 minutes"
- "Built for families who actually use their trucks"
- "Lifetime warranty"

### Where to Reach Him
- Facebook (primary)
- YouTube (research phase)
- Bronco6g forums (lurking)
- Instagram (browsing)

## Persona 3: "Practical" Pete

### Demographics
- **Age:** 45-60
- **Income:** $100-150K household
- **Location:** Midwest, Texas, Southeast
- **Vehicle:** Jeep Gladiator or full-size truck (F-150, Silverado)

### Psychographics
- Uses truck for work AND play
- Practical, no-nonsense mindset
- Veteran or first responder likely
- Values American-made products
- Loyal once he finds brands he trusts

### Behaviors
- Buys based on function, not looks
- Reads specs and reviews carefully
- Price-sensitive but will pay for quality
- Word-of-mouth driven
- Skeptical of marketing hype

### Goals
- Truck that works hard during the week
- Ready for hunting/fishing weekends
- Organized and efficient cargo management
- Products that won't fail when needed

### Pain Points
- Tired of cheap accessories that break
- Needs serious tie-downs for work gear
- Wants bed organization that actually works
- Hates complicated installs

### How EZ4X4 Helps
- Heavy-duty construction = real work use
- High weight ratings = haul anything
- Simple install = less time, more doing
- Lifetime warranty = one and done

### Marketing Messages That Resonate
- "Built to work"
- "1,500 lb capacity"
- "No gimmicks, just quality"
- "American engineered"
- "Military/first responder discount"

### Where to Reach Him
- Facebook (primary)
- Google search (when researching)
- Forums (hunting, fishing, trade-specific)
- Email (once subscribed)

## Targeting Implications

### Facebook/Instagram Ads
- **Wayne:** Target Bronco owner groups, family camping interests, ages 35-45
- **Olivia:** Target overlanding interests, outdoor adventure, ages 28-38, Instagram focus
- **Pete:** Target work truck interests, hunting/fishing, ages 45-60, Facebook focus
- **Nate:** Target new Bronco/Jeep groups, ages 25-35, TikTok/Instagram focus

### Email Segmentation
- Segment by vehicle type
- Segment by past purchases
- Segment by engagement level

### Content Strategy
- Product-focused for Pete and Wayne
- Lifestyle/adventure for Olivia
- Education/guidance for Nate

### Pricing Strategy
- Full price for Olivia (value quality)
- Discount-responsive: Wayne, Pete, Nate
- Bundle-focused for Nate
