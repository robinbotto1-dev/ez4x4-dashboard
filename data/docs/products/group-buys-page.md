## Body Copy

Running a Jeep club? Bronco meet-up group? Truck enthusiast community?

We love supporting the off-road community. That's why we offer exclusive group pricing for clubs and organizations ordering together.

### Group Buy Benefits

- **15% off** orders of 5+ units
- **20% off** orders of 10+ units  
- **Free shipping** on all group orders
- **Dedicated support** - one point of contact for your whole order
- **Flexible delivery** - ship to one address or individual members

## FAQ

**Q: What's the minimum order size?**
A: 5 units of any product(s) to qualify for group pricing.

**Q: Can we mix and match products?**
A: Absolutely. 5 different members ordering 5 different products still qualifies.

**Q: How long is the pricing valid?**
A: Group quotes are valid for 30 days.

**Q: Can we do multiple group buys?**
A: Yes! Clubs can organize as many group buys as they want.
