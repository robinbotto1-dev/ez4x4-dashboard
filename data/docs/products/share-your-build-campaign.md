## What We're Looking For

- Photos of your vehicle with EZ4X4 products installed

## What You Get

**Every submission:**
- Chance to be featured on our social media (100K+ followers)
- Entered into monthly gear giveaway

**Featured builds:**
- Spotlight on our website
- $50 store credit
- Exclusive "Featured Builder" badge

**Build of the Month:**
- $250 in free EZ4X4 products
- Featured in our email newsletter
- Custom shoutout video from the team

## Submission Form Fields

1. Name
2. Email
3. Instagram/TikTok handle (optional)
4. Vehicle (Year, Make, Model)
5. EZ4X4 Products Installed (checkboxes)
6. Photo Upload (up to 5 images)
7. Tell us about your build (text area)
8. Consent checkbox: "I give EZ4X4 permission to use my photos for marketing"
