# Forum Strategy Playbook

## Best Practices (from Jay's call)

### Posting Schedule
- **Best time:** Monday 8AM Pacific (11AM Eastern)
- **Peak window:** 8AM-1PM Pacific weekdays
- **Weekends:** Lower activity

### Thread Strategy
- Always start NEW threads for giveaways
- Post in BOTH technical sections AND sponsor section
- Link to existing positive review threads for credibility

### Content That Works
- **Photos:** iPhone quality is fine
- **Videos:** 50MB limit, YouTube embed works
- **Community discussions:** Not just product-focused
- **Themed weekly posts:** "Freedom Panel Friday", "Top Off Tuesday"

### Examples to Follow
- **Wolf Box** - Most successful sponsor
  - Active on 10+ sites
  - Tons of giveaways
  - Posts discussion threads (weather, general topics)
  - Search "wolfbox installs" for examples
- **3W Liners** - Floor mats, same strategy
- **Oracle Lighting** - Themed weekly threads

### Rules to Remember
- NEVER comment on other sponsors' threads
- Can't coerce positive reviews
- Fake accounts will get caught
- Report competitor fake accounts to Jay
