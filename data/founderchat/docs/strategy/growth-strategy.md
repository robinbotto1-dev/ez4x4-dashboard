## Current State

## Positioning

### Current
"Paid community and alert system for founders"

### Recommended
"Your unfair advantage for startup opportunities"

**Why:** Shifts from feature-focused to outcome-focused. Founders don't want "alerts" - they want to WIN opportunities.

---

## Value Proposition Canvas

### Jobs to Be Done
1. Find relevant accelerators/grants/programs
2. Never miss application deadlines
3. Get insider intel on which programs are worth it
4. Connect with founders who've been through programs
5. Improve odds of acceptance

### Pains
- Information scattered across dozens of sites
- Deadlines missed = opportunities lost
- No way to know which programs are actually good
- Application process is lonely/confusing
- Most grant databases are for nonprofits

### Gains
- One place for all opportunities
- Proactive deadline alerts
- Peer recommendations
- Application support
- Community of people in same boat

## Growth Channels

### Tier 1: High Intent (Focus Here First)

**1. Twitter/X**
- Target founders complaining about missing deadlines
- "Deadlines This Week" threads (public value, drives awareness)
- Quote tweet accelerator announcements with added context
- Engage with startup Twitter regularly

**2. Product Hunt**
- Launch the alert system as a product
- Time it around major accelerator deadline seasons
- Get founders in community to upvote/comment

**3. Indie Hackers**
- Post about building Founder Chat
- Cross-post deadline roundups
- Engage in founder communities

### Tier 2: Discovery

**4. SEO/Content**
- "Best accelerators for [industry] 2026"
- "YC alternatives for [stage] startups"
- "Grants for [type] founders"
- Deadline calendar pages (evergreen)

**5. Reddit**
- r/startups, r/entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness
- Provide genuine value, not just promo
- Answer questions about accelerators

**6. LinkedIn**
- Founder-focused content
- Accelerator alumni networks
- Startup ecosystem posts

### Tier 3: Partnerships

**7. Accelerator Partnerships**
- Offer to promote their deadlines
- Get early access for members
- Alumni referral programs

**8. Startup Tool Bundles**
- Partner with other founder tools
- Cross-promotion opportunities

## Funnel Strategy

```
Awareness → Interest → Trial → Conversion → Retention
```

### Awareness
- Free "Deadlines This Week" newsletter
- Twitter threads
- SEO content

### Interest
- Free resources (deadline calendar, accelerator comparison)
- Discord server preview (limited channels)

### Trial
- 7-day free trial of full access
- No credit card required

### Conversion
- Highlight wins during trial
- Show what they'd miss without membership
- Annual discount option

### Retention
- Weekly wins showcase
- Active community engagement
- Continuous value delivery
