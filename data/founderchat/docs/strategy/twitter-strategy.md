## Engagement Strategy

### Accounts to Engage With
- Accelerator accounts (@ycombinator, @techstars, etc.)
- Founder accounts in target demographic
- Startup media (@techcrunch, @axios, etc.)
- VC accounts who announce portfolio deadlines
- Other founder community accounts

### Engagement Tactics
1. **Quote tweet** accelerator announcements with added context
2. **Reply** to founders asking about programs with helpful info

## Growth Tactics

### 1. The Monday Thread Ritual
Post "Deadlines This Week" every Monday without fail. Consistency builds expectation. People will start looking for it.

### 2. Quote Tweet Strategy
When accelerators announce deadlines, quote tweet with:
- Added context/tips
- Who it's best for
- Related opportunities

This gets you in front of their audience.

### 3. Founder Celebration
When a founder announces getting into a program:
- Congratulate publicly
- Ask if they'd share their story
- Tag Founder Chat as resource for others

### 4. The "I would have missed this" Hook
Reframe every opportunity as: "Most founders don't know about this..."

Creates FOMO and positions you as the insider source.

### 5. Screenshot Social Proof
Screenshot DMs (with permission) of members saying:
- "I wouldn't have known about this without FC"
- "Just got accepted!"
- "The community feedback helped my app"
