# 🏔️ South Park Commons Guide

## What is South Park Commons?
South Park Commons (SPC) is a community and fellowship for builders in the "-1 to 0" phase - the stage before you've even started a company. It's a home for people exploring ideas, testing hypotheses, and building conviction before taking the leap.

## Two Programs

### 1. Member Residency
For builders in open-ended exploration mode:
- Interested in learning and idea testing
- Building relationships with other builders
- Currently employed or actively exploring
- Planning to leave your job within 6 months

### 2. Founder Fellowship
A guided path for committed founders:
- **Investment:** $1M
- **Equity:** 7%
- Committed to starting a venture-scale company
- Building conviction in an idea
- Partner mentorship and small peer cohorts
- No set deadlines or demo days

## Why SPC?

### The -1 to 0 Problem
Most programs help with 0 to 1 (building your first product) or 1 to N (scaling). SPC focuses on -1 to 0 - the messy, uncertain phase where you're:
- Exploring what to work on
- Testing ideas without commitment
- Building conviction in a direction
- Finding collaborators

### The Community
- Experienced operators from top companies
- Technical founders and builders
- Active peer support network
- No pressure to have an idea yet

### What Makes SPC Different
1. **No demo day pressure** - Explore at your own pace
2. **Peer-first** - Community of equals, not lectures
3. **Builder-focused** - Technical and product people
4. **Long-term relationships** - Many stay connected for years

## What SPC Looks For

### Strong Candidates
- **Strong technical or operational background**
- Worked at top companies or built impressive projects
- Curious and intellectually honest
- Good at building relationships

### Stage Requirements
- Based in SF or willing to relocate
- Open to full-time exploration (for Fellowship)
- Genuinely uncertain about what to build (that's the point!)

### What They Don't Require
- An idea
- A co-founder
- Previous startup experience
- Revenue or traction

## The SPC Experience

### What You Get
1. **Physical space** in San Francisco to work
2. **Structured programming** and workshops
3. **Peer cohorts** for accountability and support
4. **Mentorship** from SPC partners and alumni
5. **Network** of exceptional builders

### Typical Activities
- Weekly cohort sessions
- 1:1 conversations with partners
- Community events and dinners
- Expert sessions on specific topics
- Informal co-working and collaboration

### The Investment (Founder Fellowship)
- $1M investment
- 7% equity
- No forced timelines
- Support as long as you need it

## Application Tips

### What Makes a Strong Application
1. **Your background** - Where have you worked? What have you built?
2. **Why now?** - What's driving your exploration?
3. **Intellectual curiosity** - What problems fascinate you?
4. **Community fit** - Are you someone others want to work with?

### What to Emphasize
- Your unique experiences and skills
- Why you're entering exploration mode
- The kinds of problems that interest you
- Your approach to learning and building

### What to Avoid
- Pretending you have conviction when you don't
- Over-indexing on a specific idea
- Being vague about your background
- Underselling your experience

## SPC vs Other Programs

| Aspect | SPC Fellowship | YC | Sequoia Arc |
|--------|----------------|-----|-------------|
| Stage | -1 to 0 | 0 to 1+ | Seed |
| Investment | $1M | $500K | $1M |
| Need idea? | No | Yes | Yes |
| Duration | Flexible | 3 months | 4 days |
| Demo Day | No | Yes | No |
| Focus | Exploration | Building | Building |

## Who SPC is NOT For

Be honest with yourself:
- If you already have strong conviction → Apply to YC/Sequoia instead
- If you want structure and deadlines → Consider other accelerators
- If you're not in/willing to move to SF → Look at remote programs
- If you want a lecture series → This is peer-based

## Notable Alumni & Community

SPC has helped launch:
- Multiple successful startups (often stay stealth initially)
- Strong network of technical leaders
- Community members from Stripe, Google, Meta, etc.

## Pro Tips

1. **Be honest about your stage** - They value authenticity
2. **SF presence matters** - The community is in-person
3. **Network first** - Meet SPC members before applying
4. **It's okay to not know** - That's literally the point
5. **Think about community** - Can you contribute, not just take?

## Final Thoughts

SPC is perfect if you:
- Are at a career inflection point
- Want to explore without pressure
- Value community and peer support
- Are willing to embrace uncertainty
- Want funding without typical accelerator constraints

It's NOT for you if you:
- Already know exactly what you want to build
- Need structure and deadlines to function
- Can't commit to being in San Francisco
- Want a traditional accelerator experience

**Good luck with your exploration! 🏔️**
