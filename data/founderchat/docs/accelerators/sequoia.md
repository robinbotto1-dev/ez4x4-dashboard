# 🌲 Sequoia Arc Guide

## What is Sequoia Arc?
Arc is Sequoia's bi-annual open call for pre-seed and seed stage founders. It's their way of finding outlier founders outside their usual network. If you're accepted, you get Sequoia backing and access to the Arc Intensive, their 4-day company building program.

## The Deal
- **Investment:** $1M on uncapped SAFE
- **Equity:** ~7% (varies based on terms)
- **Program:** Arc Intensive (4 days)
- **Location:** San Francisco (hybrid)
- **Batches:** Spring and Fall (bi-annual)

## Why Sequoia?
For 50+ years, Sequoia has backed legendary companies at the earliest stages:
- Apple (pre-revenue)
- Nvidia (just the founding team)
- Google, WhatsApp, Airbnb, Stripe
- Recent: Wiz, Ramp, Notion

Having Sequoia on your cap table opens doors everywhere - talent, customers, and follow-on investors all take your calls differently.

## What They're Looking For

### Founder Qualities
- **Exceptional ambition** - Building for the next decade, not just the next quarter
- **Technical depth** - Strong technical founding team preferred
- **Speed** - Can you move faster than the competition?
- **Clarity** - Can you articulate the opportunity crisply?

### Company Stage
- Pre-seed to seed stage
- Working full-time on the startup
- Ideally based in SF or willing to be present

### The Opportunity
- Large market potential
- Unique insight or angle
- Strong product-market fit signal (or clear path to it)

## The Arc Intensive

Once funded, you participate in the Arc Intensive - 4 days of workshops distilling 50+ years of Sequoia's company-building expertise.

### Workshop Topics
1. **Founder & Culture Story** - Your motivations and values
2. **Customer Story** - Who you're building for and why
3. **Product Story** - What you're building and why it wins
4. **GTM Story** - How you're different (not just better)
5. **Business Story** - How you'll create lasting value
6. **PMF Workshop** - Finding product-market fit

### Speakers Include

## Application Tips

### Make Your Application Stand Out
1. **Lead with the problem** - What's broken and why does it matter?
2. **Show your unique insight** - What do you see that others don't?
3. **Demonstrate velocity** - How fast are you moving?
4. **Be specific** - Numbers and examples > generalizations

### What to Emphasize
- Why you're uniquely positioned to win
- Your founding team's strengths
- Evidence of customer pull
- Speed of execution

### What to Avoid
- Buzzwords without substance
- Vague market sizing
- Overselling before showing
