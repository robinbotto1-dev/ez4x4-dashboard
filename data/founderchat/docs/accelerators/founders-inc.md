# 🏗️ Founders Inc Guide

## What is Founders Inc?
Founders Inc (F.Inc) is a community-driven founder space in San Francisco, built for builders who are serious about creating something that matters. Located at Fort Mason, it's a 42,000 sq ft space buzzing with ambition, ideas, and talented builders from all over the world.

## The Vision
> "This is the space I dreamed of having when I was 18 — just tinkering, exploring, and building things with friends, fueled by curiosity and passion. A place where we could experiment freely, supported by a community of people who deeply believed in us."

F.Inc is often the **first believer** in the bold ideas founders bring to the table.

## What They Offer

### The Space
- **42,000 sq ft** at Fort Mason, San Francisco
- Co-working space designed for builders
- Event spaces for community gatherings
- Resources for prototyping and building

### The Community
- Diverse founders across multiple verticals
- Active peer support and collaboration
- Regular events and programming
- Access to mentors and advisors

### Areas of Focus
F.Inc welcomes founders building in:
- **AI** - All applications
- **AR/VR** - Immersive experiences
- **B2B** - Enterprise and SMB software
- **Hardware** - Physical products
- **Content Creation** - Tools and platforms
- **Robotics** - Automation and beyond
- *"It doesn't matter what you're building. What matters is that you're building."*

## The F.Inc Philosophy

### On Failure
> "It's more than just getting your first check from us. It's about having the space and the support to fail, get back up, and keep going."

### On Resilience
> "A founder's journey isn't defined by initial funding, monthly revenue, or even early product-market fit. It's defined by resilience, by the willingness to try again, and by the strength of the community standing beside you."

### On Community
The community is central to everything at F.Inc. You're not just getting office space - you're joining a network of ambitious builders who support each other through the ups and downs.

## Investment
F.Inc offers investment to select founders in their community, but specifics vary by situation. The focus is on being an early believer and supporter rather than a traditional accelerator model.

## Who F.Inc is For

### Good Fits
- Builders who want to be around other builders
- Early-stage founders seeking community
- Technical people who like to "tinker"
- Those who value resilience and long-term thinking
- People who can physically be in San Francisco

### Not the Right Fit If
- You want a structured accelerator program
- You need remote/distributed options
- You're looking for guaranteed investment
- You want a traditional VC relationship
