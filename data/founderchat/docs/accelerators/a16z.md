# 🏎️ a16z SPEEDRUN Guide

## What is SPEEDRUN?
SPEEDRUN is Andreessen Horowitz's (a16z) accelerator program focused on games and consumer companies. It's designed to help founders move fast and build generational companies with the full backing of a16z's resources and network.

## The Deal
- **Investment:** Up to $1M
- **Equity:** ~7% (post-money SAFE)
- **Duration:** 10-12 weeks per cohort
- **Location:** San Francisco (in-person)
- **Focus:** Games, Consumer, Entertainment

## Why SPEEDRUN?

### What You Get
1. **Capital** - Up to $1M to fuel your growth
2. **Operational Support** - Dedicated team covering:
   - Talent & recruiting
   - Go-to-market strategy
   - Marketing & creator partnerships
   - HR & visa support
   - Capital network introductions
3. **Community** - 600+ founders actively supporting each other
4. **a16z Brand** - Opens doors with talent, partners, and future investors

### The a16z Difference
a16z is known for being founder-friendly and providing serious operational support. Unlike traditional VCs, they have dedicated teams for every aspect of company building.

## What They're Looking For

### Focus Areas
- **Games** - All platforms and genres
- **Consumer apps** - Social, productivity, entertainment
- **Entertainment** - Content, media, creator tools
- **Consumer-facing AI** - Tools that delight end users

### Founder Qualities
- Strong founding team with relevant experience
- Deep understanding of your market
- Ability to move fast and iterate
- Passion for the space (not just chasing trends)

### Stage
- Pre-seed or seed stage
- Strong idea with early validation
- Ready to go full-time

## Cohort Structure

### Recent Cohort (SR006)
- **Dates:** January 26 - April 12, 2026
- **Location:** San Francisco
- **Format:** In-person program with regular sessions

### What Happens During SPEEDRUN
- Weekly sessions with a16z partners and operators
- Expert workshops on specific topics
- Community events with other founders
- 1:1 support from the SPEEDRUN team
- Demo day at the end

## Application Tips

### What Makes a Strong Application
1. **Show, don't tell** - Demo your product, share metrics
2. **Know your market** - Deep insight into games/consumer space
3. **Demonstrate velocity** - How fast are you shipping?
4. **Unique angle** - What do you see that others miss?

### Portfolio Fit
Look at SPEEDRUN's existing portfolio for pattern matching:
- Games across mobile, PC, console
- Social and community apps
- Creator tools and platforms
- Consumer AI applications

### The Interview
If selected, be ready to:
- Demo your product live
- Discuss your vision for the company
- Talk about your team and why you're the ones to build this
- Show passion and deep knowledge of the space
